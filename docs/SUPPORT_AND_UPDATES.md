# Support and update policy template

Replace every bracketed field before issuing this document to a buyer.

## Included support

- Delivery assistance for [number] days after acceptance.
- Security defect fixes for the delivered version during [period].
- Response target: [business hours and timezone].
- Contact channel: [support email or portal].

## Not included unless separately purchased

- Buyer-specific feature development.
- Buyer branding/design beyond environment configuration.
- Third-party provider charges or provider support.
- Data migration, content entry, SEO, advertising or legal compliance advice.
- Emergency work outside the agreed support window.

## Updates

The seller may maintain a master release line and publish changelogged updates. Each buyer decides whether to adopt an update after reviewing migration notes and backup requirements. Updates do not transfer ownership of the master IP.

## Buyer responsibilities

The buyer owns its branding, customer data, domains, credentials, payment-account configuration, legal pages and compliance obligations. The buyer must keep provider accounts paid, retain backups and apply security updates according to the agreed service level.
